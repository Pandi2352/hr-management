## 🎯 Release 2 Scope Summary

### 1. What is IN Release 2:
- ✅ Digital Onboarding Checklists (HR verification, IT provisioning, Manager goals, Candidate self-service).
- ✅ Probation Management (Automated 30/60/90-day review alerts, manager rating forms, confirmations, extensions).
- ✅ Lifecycle Transitions (Promotions, Department Transfers, Manager reassignments with historical audit timeline).
- ✅ Centralized Polymorphic Approval Engine (Multi-tier hierarchical manager chains, delegation, comments, rejection logic).
- ✅ Employee Requests (Profile changes, address updates, bank account changes, employment bonafide letters).
- ✅ Company Policies (PDF viewer with digital acknowledgment tracking).
- ✅ Holiday Calendars (Multiple regional calendars assigned by employee location).
- ✅ Offboarding & Exit Management (Resignation submission, notice period calculator, multi-department clearance sign-offs, confidential exit interview).

### 2. What is DEFERRED to Subsequent Releases:
- ⏳ *Attendance punch clock, shift scheduling, and leave balance engine* $\to$ **Release 3**
- ⏳ *Performance appraisals, OKRs, and 360 review cycles* $\to$ **Release 4**
- ⏳ *Recruitment & Applicant Tracking System (ATS)* $\to$ **Release 5**
